# Third-party sources

- EnvHarness is Apache-2.0 licensed. Bundled source and license are in pipeline/vendor/envharness-main. Upstream https://github.com/google-research/envharness.
- LoopTool judgement helpers retain their original source and SHA-256 provenance in pipeline/looptool_vendor/source.json. Upstream https://github.com/DeepExperience/LoopTool. The copied helper retains its source header.
- DeepSeek Harness is installed as deepseek-harness-sdk and deepseek-harness-runtime-bin. Upstream https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness.
- Terminal-Bench-Evo task materials are obtained separately from the EvoArena authors. Upstream https://github.com/Aiden0526/EvoArena.
- ReasoningBank informs memory extraction prompts. Upstream https://github.com/google-research/reasoning-bank.
